QR Generator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

QR Generator is an offline-first QR code and barcode generator published by Rs Bd Soft. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information and files processed by the app

Text entered to create a code and workbook files selected for batch generation are processed on your device. QR Generator does not send this content to an app server. The app does not require an account.

When you choose to save a generated image to your gallery, the app requests the Android media access needed for that action and writes the image to the gallery. When you choose to share an image or ZIP, Android opens the system share sheet and transfers the selected file to the app you choose. The receiving app's handling of the file is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

The current release does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. This statement will be reviewed against the final release dependencies.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not maintain an online account or app-operated server containing your generated codes. Files you explicitly save, download, or share are managed by your device or the receiving app. You can remove saved files using your device's file or gallery tools.

Children's privacy

QR Generator is not specifically directed to children. It should not be presented as a children's app unless it has been reviewed for and complies with Google Play Families requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
