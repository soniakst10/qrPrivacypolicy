Privacy Policy

App: Qr Code Genaretor

Developer: Rs Bd Soft

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Information the app processes

Qr Code Genaretor lets you enter text and create QR codes or Code 128 barcodes. The text and generated codes are processed on your device. The app does not send this content to a server, collect personal information, require an account, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing

If you choose Share, Android shows the system share menu so you can select another app. The code image and its text are then provided to the app you select. That app's own privacy policy and practices apply to anything you choose to share with it. Qr Code Genaretor does not receive a copy of content handled by the selected app.

Storage and security

The app does not keep a history of generated codes. Text is held in the app while you use it and is removed from the app's in-memory state when you close it. The app requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, or photo library. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
